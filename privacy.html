Privacy Policy — เฮียคัดมาแล้ว Uploader

Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. What data we access

When the channel owner connects their TikTok account, the Tool receives:

  • basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) through the user.info.basic scope;
  • permission to upload videos as drafts through the video.upload scope.

    • 2. How we use it

    The data is used only to upload our own videos to our own TikTok account and to show which account is connected.

    3. Storage

    Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

    4. Other users

    The Tool does not collect data from any other TikTok users.

    5. Deleting data

    The owner can disconnect the Tool at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), and delete the locally stored tokens.
